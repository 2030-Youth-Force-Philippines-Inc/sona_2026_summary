Executive takeaway

Reported relief, energy and governance actions now need measured outcomes, while resilience delivery and four unaddressed SDGs warrant closer attention before 2030. Congress’s levers are the 2027 budget, the tax and energy measures it was asked to consider, and oversight hearings.
