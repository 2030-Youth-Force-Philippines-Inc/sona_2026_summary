    Method and limits

    Source

    The 2026 SONA: Summary and SDG Coverage Report (July 2026), from the official transcript, Rappler and the Presidential Communications Office text, plus the 2025 SDG Youth Agenda (YFPH).

    Classification

    Extensive: named programs, figures or targets. Brief: in passing. Not covered: absent or incidental.

    Evidence limits

    Every figure is a statement made in the address and is unverified. Suggested indicators are standard UN SDG indicator names; no values are asserted.

    Neutrality

    Institutional, not partisan. No assessment of individuals or motives; proposed measures are not guaranteed solutions.
